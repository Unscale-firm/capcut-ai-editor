# EDITING_CRAFT.md — b-roll & insert craft — **ADS ONLY**
Applies to every ad cue table in ADS_EDIT_MAP.md. Does NOT apply to the VSL: the VSL is cut exactly per 73_VSL_Edit_Map.md as written — do not re-pass or restyle VSL work against this doc.

The job of every insert: **prove or amplify the sentence being spoken, then get out.**
If an insert doesn't do one of those two things, it's noise — cut it.

## 1 · Entrance timing
- The insert lands **ON the noun that names it** ("Spiralyze" → the Spiralyze proof, not a second later). Same for pops: exactly on the spoken word.
- Enter on phrase/breath boundaries, never mid-word.
- Hooks (ads): the FIRST frame is already in motion — start inside a punch-in or with a pop mid-build. Static first frames die in the feed.

## 2 · Exit timing (leave before interest dies)
- Stills: 1.5–2.5s. Clips: 2–4s. Full-screen takeovers (testimonial with audio): as long as the speaker earns, then hard out.
- Return to the speaker's face BEFORE the next claim starts — the face sells the next sentence, the insert sold the last one.
- Stacked pops go out TOGETHER on the beat after the last one lands, not one by one.

## 3 · Stills must move (Ken Burns discipline)
- Every still gets a 2–5% push or drift. Direction: push IN for weight (receipts, portraits), drift LATERAL for context (wide shots, pages).
- **House distinction — memory vs evidence:** polaroid frame + soft entrance = MEMORY (family photos, era flashbacks). Full-bleed raw + hard cut = EVIDENCE (receipts, rejections, grind selfies). Never swap the two treatments.

## 4 · Audio grammar
- Muted tiles/inserts: the speaker's VO runs CONTINUOUS underneath (L-cut feel) — the viewer must never feel the audio hiccup.
- Speaking inserts (testimonials, reaction montages): full audio takeover, music ducks −12dB or out, VO pauses. One speaking insert per ad max.
- Silence is a weapon: 0.3s dead air before a punchline, dip-to-black as a punch. Use once or twice per video, never more.
- Every pop gets a subtle tick/whoosh; big cards get a hit. No sound = the pop didn't happen.

## 5 · Rhythm (the 3–4 second rule)
- Something changes every 3–4s: angle switch, punch-in step (5–10%), insert, or pop landing/clearing. Pops count as changes — pop-dense passages need fewer camera moves.
- Cut angles on phrase boundaries. Punch-ins land on emphasis words.
- Register maps to density: offer/CTA = dense; story = sparser, slower push-ins; dignity beats (father, promise-to-son) = NOTHING on screen, locked or slow-push camera only.

## 6 · Scent + rotation
- B-roll must match the words at that second (scent). Never re-use a signature asset outside its owner beat: buying-crescendo clips belong to the VSL cold-open + F-08 only.
- Across an ad set, ROTATE intros, tiles, and mosaics so retargeted viewers never see the same combination twice.
- Callback ceiling: any single proof asset appears MAX 3 times across one video; past that it's wallpaper.

## 7 · Text pops
- Exact spoken words only, ≤5 words, DM Sans on Dark Ops, accent = Orange Ember.
- In/out animation ≤200ms; one pop owns the screen at a time (stacks build sequentially).
- Numbers beat adjectives: pop the figure, not the feeling.

## 8 · 9:16 specifics (ads)
- Face in top ⅔; Meta UI eats the bottom ~15% — nothing critical there.
- Inserts: ≤40% frame as PiP, or full-frame takeover. Nothing in between (half-frame reads as broken).
- Punch into 4K sources freely; 1080p sources max 115% scale.
- Burned captions always (sound-off viewing), keywords highlighted.

## 9 · Compliance rails (non-negotiable)
- Receipts (contracts, payments, personal emails, rejection letters) = VSL only, never paid ads.
- Family/Rayan material = VSL only, founder-assigned slots only.
- No stock footage anywhere. Real assets or brand-styled text/graphics.
- Redaction is done upstream — editor uses files AS SUPPLIED, never restores anything.
- Claims gated on substantiation (income figures, layoff numbers) stay out until the compliance file confirms.

## 10 · The test for every finished cut
Play it muted: does the story still track from pops + inserts alone? Play it audio-only: does anything on screen contradict the words? Both must pass.
